# Email Masking

Email masking hides usernames by replacing them with a special character, such as an asterisk (`*`), while preserving the email domain intact.

---

## Examples

| Original value              | Transformed value             |
|-----------------------------|-------------------------------|
| robert_brownie@tech.gov.sg  | **************@tech.gov.sg    |
| melissa@gmail.com           | *******@gmail.com             |
