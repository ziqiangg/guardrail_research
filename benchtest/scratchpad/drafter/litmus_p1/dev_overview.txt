Overview
Litmus
PROOF OF CONCEPT
Litmus is a Testing-as-a-Service (TaaS) platform that helps development teams test Generative AI application safety and security. It enables seamless AI testing within CI/CD pipelines and web apps, providing real-time insights to manage evolving AI risks and inform decisions.
View Features
Key Benefits
Comprehensive AI safety and security testing
Ensures that Generative AI applications in the public sector meet the highest safety and security standards.
Seamless CI/CD integration
Allows for near real-time identification of risks by integrating directly with application CI/CD processes.
Contextualised risk assessment
Provides tailored risk analysis specific to the use cases of AI in government services.
Automated and frequent safety checks
Conducts regular, automated safety assessments to maintain ongoing reliability and security.
Customisable testing scenarios
Adapts to diverse government AI use cases like chatbots, document processing, and policy analysis.
Compliance with security standards
Follows government-specific security and data protection standards, ensuring reliability for public sector applications.
