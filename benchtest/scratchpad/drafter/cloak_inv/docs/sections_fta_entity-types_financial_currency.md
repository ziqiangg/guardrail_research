# Currency

---

### Default setting:

- REPLACE values with a symbol or a 3-char symbol before or after the digits with `<CURRENCY>` tag.

---

### Example:

| Original Value              | Anonymised Value        |
|-----------------------------|------------------------|
| I have $5000 USD.           | I have `<CURRENCY>`.    |
| May I have ¥500,000 please? | May I have `<CURRENCY>` please? |
