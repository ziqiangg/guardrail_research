# Date & Time

---

### Default setting:

- REPLACE dates and times with a `<DATE_TIME>` tag.

---

### Example:

| Original Value                          | Anonymised Value                     |
|---------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| I believe it is 1800 GMT+8 in Singapore now. | I believe it is `<DATE_TIME>` in Singapore now. |
| Would 22 January 1997, 10:25AM be good for you? | Would `<DATE_TIME>` be good for you? |
