# Disease

## Scenario  
User wants to anonymise **names of medical conditions**, which are **not pre-existing entities** in Cloak or other common NLP models.

---

## Prompts  

![disease-1](_resources/disease-1.png)

*Defining the DISEASE custom entity with a description covering acronyms, misspellings, and variants, along with a first labelled example.*

![disease-2](_resources/disease-2.png)

*Additional examples showing tagged disease names such as Bronchitic COPD, Emphyzma, M-Sclerosis, and SPMS.*

---

## Output  

![disease-3](_resources/disease-3.png)

*Anonymisation output replacing detected disease names in a patient record with DISEASE tags.*
