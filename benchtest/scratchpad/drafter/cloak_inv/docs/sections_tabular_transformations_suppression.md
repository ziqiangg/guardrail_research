# Suppression

Suppression hides values by replacing them with a special character, such as an asterisk (*).

Suppression is typically applied when the values are deemed too risky to disclose (e.g., outlier values) or do not meet the [k-anonymity](/sections/tabular/privacy-risk-score/k-anonymity-test.md) requirement.

---

## Examples

| Original value | Transformed value |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Pneumonia      | *                 |
| 120414         | *                 |
