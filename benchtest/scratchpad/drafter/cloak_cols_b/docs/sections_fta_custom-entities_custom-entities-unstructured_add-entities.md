# How to Add Entities

## Step 1: Select "Anonymisation Settings" on Free-Text Anonymisation Project Page

![add-entities-1](_resources/add-entities-1.png)

*The "Anonymisation Settings" button on the project page.*

---
## Step 2: Navigate to "Custom entities" under Anonymisation Settings

![add-entities-2](_resources/add-entities-2.png)

*The Anonymisation Settings drawer with the "Custom entities" tab selected.*

---

## Step 3: Select "Add Custom Entity" in Custom Entities tab

![add-entities-3](_resources/add-entities-3.png)

*The "Add a custom entity" button (with purple border).*

---

## Step 4: Name your Custom Entity, select "Large-Language Model (LLM) based" as your approach, and press "Add entity".

![add-entities-4](_resources/add-entities-4.png ':size=350x')

*The "Add a custom entity" box with the entity named "DISEASE" and the option "Large-Language Model (LLM) based" selected.*

---

## Step 5: Input your Definition and Examples
We strongly recommend reading our [guide on how to write Definition and Examples](/sections/fta/custom-entities/custom-entities-unstructured/write-prompts.md). 

![add-entities-5](_resources/add-entities-5.png)

*The "Custom entities" tab showing the "DISEASE" entity and its "Definition" and "Examples" fields.*

---

## Step 6: Label Your Examples  

![add-entities-6](_resources/add-entities-6.png)

*Typing "Bipolar Depression" into the "Output for Example 1" field before pressing "Enter" on the keyboard.*

![add-entities-7](_resources/add-entities-7.png)

*The labelled output "Bipolar Depression" shown as a tag beneath the example.*

---

## Step 7: Close the drawer and save your Definition and Examples as a template (Optional)
This will allow you to apply the same setting on new projects. 

![add-entities-8](_resources/add-entities-8.png)

*The "Save Template" button.*

---

## Step 8: Select "Preview"

![add-entities-9](_resources/add-entities-9.png)

*The "Preview" button to the left of the "Start Anonymisation" button.*

---

## Step 9: Your data is transformed!
To run the job on your full dataset, select "Start anonymisation".

You may need to revise Definition and Examples based on the performance you observe in your output. Read our [guide on writing Definitions and Examples here](/sections/fta/custom-entities/custom-entities-unstructured/write-prompts.md). 

![add-entities-10](_resources/add-entities-10.png)

*The project page showing the anonymised output with disease names replaced by the DISEASE tag and the "Start Anonymisation" button on the upper right in green.*
