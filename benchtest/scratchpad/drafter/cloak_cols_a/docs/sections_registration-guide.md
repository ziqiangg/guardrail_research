# Registration Guide

For users who register from [Cloak's website](https://www.cloak.gov.sg/register), there are severals fields that you will need to fill in as these fields are required when registering a Techpass account.

- If you are a WOG user, please sign in via WOG AD.
- For vendors who register, you will receive an email from Techpass to complete the onboarding. Once you complete the onboarding, Techpass will send your Techpass email (ending with techpass.gov.sg) via your registered email and the initial password via your registered SMS.

**Registration Flow:**

![Registration page](_resources/registration-guide-1.png ':size=600x')

*Registration page*

- **Email address:** Valid work email address with the domain whitelisted on our system. This is required as Techpass will send the onboarding to this email.
- **First name:** First name and can contain letters, dash, dot and spaces.
- **Last name:** Last name and can contain letters, dash, dot and spaces.
- **Username:** Any text where it starts and ends with alphanumeric and can contain dash, underscore and dot.
- **Mobile Number:** A valid registered Singapore phone number. This is required as Techpass will send the initial password to you via this number.
- **Organization and Sub-Team and Role:** A brief summary of the team's scope.
- **Use-case:** A brief summary on why the team is using Cloak.

Once you click register, it will submit the request to Cloak's Ops Team. Our Ops Team will then approve/decline the request and you will receive an email from Cloak with the subject `Cloak Account Creation Request` on your request status.

![Techpass invitation](_resources/registration-guide-2.png)

*Techpass Invitation from Cloak to Vendor*

### TECHPASS

If your request has been approved, you will also receive an onboarding email from Techpass.

![Techpass onboarding email](_resources/registration-guide-3.png)

*Techpass's onboarding email*

Click on the link to complete the registration. For more information, you can visit this [page](https://docs.developer.tech.gov.sg/docs/techpass-user-guide/sign-up-and-onboard-to-techpass) for details on the registration (step1).

Once you have completed the steps, you should receive an email Techpass and SMS from gov.sg.

![Username to login from Techpass](_resources/registration-guide-4.png)

*Username to login from Techpass*

![Password sent via SMS from gov.sg](_resources/registration-guide-5.png ':size=400x')

*SMS from GovTech containing your initial TechPass account password.*

With the username (email ending with techpass.gov.sg) and the password received from SMS, you may now login to Cloak via `Login with TechPass`.

![Username received from email](_resources/registration-guide-6.png ':size=500x')

*TechPass sign-in page where you enter your techpass.gov.sg email address.*

![Password received from SMS](_resources/registration-guide-7.png ':size=500x')

*Enter the initial password received via SMS to complete your sign-in.*

Once you managed to login, techpass will prompt you to do a password reset. For more information, you may refer to this [page](https://docs.developer.tech.gov.sg/docs/techpass-user-guide/get-invited-and-onboard-to-techpass) (step 2 onwards). Now, you should be able to see the landing page of Cloak.
