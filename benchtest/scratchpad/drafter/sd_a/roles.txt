Common permissions
Some
Sensitive Data Protection methods require permissions from other
services. For example, to interact with Cloud Storage or BigQuery,
you need the relevant permissions for those services. Additionally, all billable
methods require that the caller has the serviceusage.services.use
permission on the project being used. This permission is included in
many Sensitive Data Protection roles, such as the DLP User role
(roles/dlp.user).
