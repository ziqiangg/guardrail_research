### Q03 - What counts as self-hosted given embedder dependencies
- Product: lionguard   Phase: P0   Asked by: explorer
- Context: Only the classifier head (3.4 MB for 2 and 2.1, 1.0 MB for Lite) is GovTech's weight. LionGuard 2 needs the user's OpenAI embeddings API key and 2.1 a Gemini API key (text goes to a third party). Lite runs locally, but its embedder `google/embeddinggemma-300m` is HF-gated (manual acceptance of Google's Gemma licence). R005: columns cover what you get running the models yourselves.
- Options: (a) keep all three variants in the one column and state dependencies in R7 and inventory (c); (b) treat Lite as the self-host path and 2 and 2.1 as "needs external API" in R7 only; (c) exclude variants that need a paid third-party API.
- Asker's recommendation: (a). It matches R005 and the playbook ("open-sourced for self-hosting"). Keys and the Gemma licence acceptance are bench-design items under R019.
- Blocking? no.
