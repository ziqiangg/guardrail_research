# ≠ Exceptions

≠ Exceptions are used to manually exclude words from being detected and anonymised.

This is available at the bottom of the **Transform** drawer. Simply key in the word(s) or phase(s) which you want the tool to ignore. You may add multiple tokens under this category.

![exceptions-1](_resources/exceptions-1.png)

*The Exceptions section at the bottom of the Transform drawer, where words can be added to exclude them from anonymisation.*
