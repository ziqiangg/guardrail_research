# Location
*Countries, cities, states, and other named geographical locations (mountains, bodies of water, regions).*

---

### Default setting:

- REPLACE location names with `<LOCATION>` tag.

---

### Examples:

| Original Value | Anonymised Value |
|---|---|
| The U.K. is no longer part of Europe. | The `<LOCATION>` is no longer part of `<LOCATION>`. |
| Mt. Everest is the tallest mountain in the world. | `<LOCATION>` is the tallest mountain in the world. |
| She flew from Singapore to Tokyo last week. | She flew from `<LOCATION>` to `<LOCATION>` last week. |
| The Mekong River runs through Southeast Asia. | The `<LOCATION>` runs through `<LOCATION>`. |

### What is detected:

- Countries and territories (e.g. Singapore, the U.K., Australia)
- Cities and states (e.g. Tokyo, California, Johor Bahru)
- Geographical features (e.g. Mt. Everest, the Mekong River, the Pacific Ocean)
- Named regions (e.g. Southeast Asia, Europe, the Middle East)
